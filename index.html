The studio

Small team, mobile-first, all in.

The turul is the mythic falcon of Turkic legend — a messenger moving between the sky and the earth. We took the name because it’s how we want our games to feel: fast, sharp, and hard to forget.

We design for the way people actually play on a phone — a few minutes at a time, one hand, often in the middle of something else — and we sweat the small details that make those minutes feel good.
